# Infotainment and camera upgrade options: 2018 Stelvio 2.0T

Reference car: 2018 Alfa Romeo Stelvio 2.0T Q4, VIN ZASFAKPN5J7B88115. Written 2026-10-22 from two web-research notes made the same day. **Status: evaluation. No product chosen, nothing bought, nothing installed.** Nothing was read from or written to the car, the adapter or any server for this document.

**Owner goal (2026-10-22):** upgrade, replace or enhance the infotainment so that extra cameras can be added: the rear digital-mirror camera, a front camera, side cameras and a 360 degree view.

**This document does not recommend or endorse any product.** Several options are paid products. They are compared by objective criteria only (section 3), and no row is labelled recommended or best. The choice is the owner's.

## Evidence labels

Every claim keeps the label its source note gave it.

- **[official]**: manufacturer or installer manual or datasheet, or the FCA wiTECH knowledge base; also NHTSA documents.
- **[listing]**: a vendor or retailer page. These are sales claims, not verified.
- **[forum]**: an owner post. Most forum pages were behind a bot gate (a redirect to a paywalled host that returned HTTP 402), so these come from search-engine excerpts or summaries. Treat them as anecdote and open each thread before relying on it.
- **[inference]**: reasoning by the research note's author or by this document, not stated by any source.
- **[CUORE record]**: a reading or record already held in this repository for this car. Not re-measured here.

"Not stated" means the source is silent. "Not found" means searched for and not located; it is not proof of absence. Prices are as listed, in the currency shown. No CAD figure is sourced except the Sigma listing and possibly the Best Buy Canada listing. As an inference, CAD is roughly USD x1.35-1.40; duty, shipping and tax are extra.

### 2.2 Route B: interface boxes that keep the OEM screen

The 2018 head unit has no CarPlay in all builds (see 2.4). Boxes in this class add CarPlay / Android Auto and/or camera inputs, and leave the ETM, knob, amplifier, climate and DNA display in place. Camera inputs are CVBS (NTSC) class per the vendors that say; AHD / 1080p is not stated for any of them.

**Best-documented design: Ampire r.LiNK RL4-C3D** [official](https://pdf.ampire.de/caraudiosystems/RL4-C3D/ins_eng_RL4-C3D%20.pdf) (manual dated 26.06.2023; also an older version at https://www.manualslib.com/manual/2029733/R-Link-Rl4-C3d.html)

- Fits "Connect Radio with 6.5 inch monitor or Connect 3D navigation with 8.8 inch monitor". Lists Stelvio (949) "since 01/2017 til about 2020".
- Video inserter only: 1 CVBS rear, 1 CVBS front, 2 CVBS aftermarket sources, NTSC only. **No CarPlay/AA.** 360: not stated. Video only, so audio uses the factory AUX input or an FM modulator.
- Connection: the purple HSD+2 (LVDS) screen cable is unplugged at the head unit and re-routed through the box ("HU IN" / "TO LCD"). The Quadlock connector is also taken off the head unit and passed through a power/CAN cable. The manual warns that swapping HU IN and TO LCD "may cause dysfunction or even damage".
- Reverse trigger: the box reads CAN. Some cars send a different reverse code the box does not understand; then a relay from the reverse lamp is required. The manual says a reverse-lamp tap is not voltage-stable, so a relay or filter is recommended.
- Front camera shows for 10 s after leaving reverse (dip 1). With dip 5 OFF the box shows the factory camera in reverse; with dip 5 ON it shows its own rear camera. Switching is by the factory infotainment button or the box's keypad.
- Parking guide lines and the on-screen PDC overlay depend on CAN communication and are "not available for all vehicles".
- Standby draw 8 mA, 330 mA at 12 V active. The manual says to run a standby test after install, and to disconnect the battery and, if needed, put the car in sleep mode before installing.
- Warns that "changes/updates of the vehicle's software can cause malfunctions of the interface"; offers one year of free software updates only if the box is sent in at the owner's cost.
- Dip switches 7 and 8 select monitor timing by trial, so the screen variant is not self-detecting.
- Price: not stated.

### 2.3 Route C: replacement Android head unit

**How these connect.** The vehicle-specific Giulia/Stelvio Android screens are not a full replacement of the ETM. Per sellers and owners they replace the display, add an Android computer, and sit between the original head unit's video output and the screen:

- Sigma: "retains ALL the original car information", works with the original BOSE sound system and steering controls, "same wiring harness and plug as the factory unit... no wire cutting or coding" [listing](https://sigmacarstereo.com/alfa-romeo-giulia-stelvio-10-25-4gb-64gb-aftermarket-radio-upgrade-carstereo).
- Krando lists "original car info and NTG functions", "original joystick control of the screen", "original sound system and optical fiber" as retained [listing](https://krandoauto.com/products/krando-10-25-android-car-radio-gps-stereo-for-alfa-romeo-stelvio-2017-2018-touch-screen-head-unit-navigation-multimedia-player-system-wireless-carplay-wifi-4g).
- A Stelvioforum member: the Alibaba units "use screen integration, so you still need the Alfa infotainment to access all the car settings" [forum](https://www.stelvioforum.com/threads/upgrade-to-2020-infotainment-head-unit.17368/).
- Installer guide for the same class of kit (an interface box): remove the 48-pin Quadlock, unplug the LVDS cable; "if there is a MOST fibre optic line in the Quadlock, transfer it to the new Quadlock" [listing/installer](https://integratedautomotive.co.uk/user-intstallation-guide-retrofit-carplay-and-android-auto-into-alfa-romeo-giulia-stelvio-2016-2019/).
- The factory screen is non-touch. You cannot swap a touchscreen in from a later OEM car on a 2018-2019 (older ETM hardware) [forum](https://www.stelvioforum.com/threads/aftermarket-display-upgrade.23119/). A Giulia owner confirmed motherboard/ETM incompatibility putting an OEM MY20 8.8" screen on an older car [forum](https://www.giuliaforums.com/threads/replacing-the-6-5-screan-for-10-25-touch.50538/).
- Because the original ETM stays powered and on the bus in this design, the radio-missing U-code risk is lower than with a radio-deleting swap. The risk moves to the unit's CAN/harness tap, the Quadlock/MOST/audio path and wake/sleep behaviour. No source measured this on a Stelvio [inference].

## 4. Sequencing advice

**This section is an inference** drawn from the sources and from this car's records. It is not a source statement.

1. **Do no infotainment or CAN-tap work before the EVAP monitor proof run is finished and the 2026-10-28 dealer visit is done.** Reason: both are open tests of the car's current state. A new U-code or a changed PROXI result appearing during or just after them could not be told apart from an existing one. The EVAP proof run also forbids ECM clears and battery disconnects without a note, and some box manuals (RL4-C3D) ask for a battery disconnect or sleep-mode procedure.
2. **Until then, research and decisions only.** Collect the answers in section 6. Ask for written answers from any vendor on camera input count and type, whether the unit taps CAN-IHS or only the OEM harness, whether it keeps the ETM powered, sleep current, the reverse trigger on a Stelvio automatic, and 2018-specific knob support.
3. **Read the radio's software level from its menu and its label** (the CUORE note on U1736 already asks for this). TSB 08-029-20 "Stelvio Infotainment Update 2.0 Radio -> 002.010.041" is discussed on Stelvioforum [forum](https://www.stelvioforum.com/threads/tsb-08-029-20-stelvio-infotainment-update-2-0-radio-002-010-041.12486/) (not opened). Any radio update belongs before a third-party unit goes on, not after.
4. **Record a CUORE baseline scan before the install:** the module list and all U-codes with their status bytes, taken through a CUORE session (never a raw adapter script). Add a key-off sleep-current measurement, logged for 20-30 minutes after lock. Repeat both after the install, with the same method, and compare. A code in the "after" scan that is also in the baseline is not caused by the install.
5. **One change at a time.** Do not combine the mirror install (job 98a6aad68d72), a camera box and any radio work on the same day. The mirror plan already says to do the mirror on a separate day and to tell the dealer there is aftermarket wiring in the car.
6. **After the install:** never clear DASM, HALF, airbag or ABS codes to "tidy up"; keep the pre-install history. Note radio software version and box firmware before any later dealer work, and be ready to unplug the box [inference from the RL4-C3D manual].
7. **Tell the dealer** about any aftermarket box or wiring before further dealer work. One forum thread has a dealer blaming add-ons for a drain.

## 6. Open questions for the owner

1. **Which screen does the car have, 8.8" or 6.5"?** Several products are keyed to the size (RL4-C3D dip switches 7 and 8, IMI-1000 DIP settings, thecarplayer 6.5" or 8", INTEAR1719 6.8"/8.8"). The request for this evaluation spoke of keeping "the OEM 8.8 inch" screen, but the size is not recorded in this repository, so please confirm. Also: touch or non-touch.
2. **Does the car have ParkView (the factory rear camera) and the rear park sensors?** This decides whether a rear feed already exists on the screen.
3. **Does it have the Harman Kardon audio system?** The Quadlock may carry a MOST fibre line; Krando says it keeps the original sound system and fibre, and Harman Kardon behaviour is "not found" for every Android unit. A box that needs the AUX input also depends on what the audio path allows.
4. **What is the budget, and in which currency?** Listings run from about $250 to EUR 3,000 (section 1). Duty, shipping and tax are extra on imports. No CAD figure is sourced for most.
5. **Do you want 360?** If yes: Route A has a documented 360 set; Route B and C are vendor claims only; Route D has none stated. Do you want CarPlay / Android Auto as well, or cameras only? (Route B includes camera-only options; some CarPlay boxes are for cars with no CarPlay.)
6. **Which camera positions?** Rear (mirror plus screen), front (bumper/grille or windshield), left and right side. Mounting and wiring for a front or side camera were not researched.
7. **Who will install?** Several installs are described as physically hard (footwell trim, cubby box, unbolting the head unit); one professional install was quoted at "150".
8. **Radio symptoms and history:** any radio reboot, black screen, lost CarPlay / Bluetooth, nav fault, or any aftermarket head unit, amplifier or USB work since the radio codes of 2026-10-08 to 10-13? And the car's build date and radio software level.
9. **Dealer:** is the owner willing to ask the 2026-10-28 visit about the radio software level, the presence of a security gateway and whether a Mopar parts counter lists any camera or surround accessory for this VIN?
