# Third-party fuse tables (summary)

Data: `fuse_tables_third_party.json` (facts plus source URLs only).

Sources (both third-party, not official):
- https://www.startmycar.com/alfa-romeo/stelvio/info/fusebox/2018 (fuse types come from here)
- https://fuse-box.info/alfa-romeo/alfa-romeo-stelvio-2017-2019-fuses (box locations come from its text)

Coverage: 16 positions in two boxes, 8 each.
- Passenger compartment fuse box (Passenger Side Control Unit): below the passenger side footboard.
- Luggage compartment fuse box (Luggage Compartment Control Unit): right side of the luggage compartment behind the side cover.

Gaps: no engine-bay box, no relay tables, no slot layouts. The 16 positions match the count CUORE already holds. A French Fiat service PDF reportedly has rear box tables but returned 503. The official owner's manual could not be read (see review_and_findings.md).
