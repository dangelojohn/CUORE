# Stelvio side-view trace: photo credit

The side outline in `cuore/web/sensor_profile_svg.py` (`_SIDE_BODY`, `_SIDE_DETAIL`) is traced from:

- File: "Alfa Romeo Stelvio 154026.jpg" (2446 x 1809 px, original size)
- Page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Alfa_Romeo_Stelvio_154026.jpg
- Author: Trop86 (own work, taken 2026-04-19, Neu-Ulm; "Alfa Romeo Stelvio SUV in Neu-Ulm")
- Licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication), https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  No credit is required; it is given anyway.
- SHA-1 of the file used: 93c30eab7e07092481d15fed3edd14efbce2aef5

Caveats: the car is a grey Stelvio with the Veloce black pack (post-2020 headlamps), photographed about 8 degrees
off pure side-on with the rear nearer the camera. The photo was levelled on the two tyres and the axles pinned to
the drawing frame (x 199 / 650); nose and tail were rescaled to the frame's overhangs (nose x1.47, tail x0.89).
The side body shell is the same for 2017-2020. The photo itself is not stored in the repo.
`tools/` holds the scripts used (they read scratch paths under the Temp scratchpad).
