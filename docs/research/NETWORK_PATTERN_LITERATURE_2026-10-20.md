## 2. Known causes for this cluster (literature, by theme)

1. Security Gateway. [DOC] STAR S1708000246 Rev B, NHTSA https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2022/MC-10221422-9999.pdf: DLC and radio are the "public" side, CAN C and CAN IHS the "private" side; the SGW is the firewall and sets no fault code when it blocks aftermarket messages; with SGW not communicating wiTECH topology shows every module red; CAN C, CAN C2 and CAN IHS modules do not set codes because of an inoperative SGW; the SGW reports one DTC per bus type without saying which side; it never reports the diagnostic buses at the OBD port; a replaced SGW needs the wiTECH replacement routine and wrong calibration files cause partial or total loss of features. Model coverage of that note is not stated (it names 500L/Atlantis, Powernet, CUSW); Giorgio is not named. Companion item: 2018+ SGW overview https://kb.fcawitech.com/print-903.html. The pattern here (several modules each reporting erratic or invalid signals, no code in the ABS) is not what the SGW document predicts for an SGW fault. [INF]
2. BCM power feeds and connectors. [DOC] STAR S1808000005 (catalogue `tsb-s1808000005`, https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2020/MC-10175655-9999.pdf): no start with multiple modules not responding; inspect all power feed circuits to the BCM incl. the stand-alone 20 A fuse; for GU/Stelvio inspect fuse F82 in the rear PDC (BCM circuit A901). [DOC] STAR S1708000262 Rev A (`tsb-s1708000262`, https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2020/MC-10184414-9999.pdf): intermittent module CAN private or LIN codes (example U1008-00 at the ECM): inspect the connector for pushed-out or spread terminals and repair the terminal. Model list for these STAR notes is not in the text.
3. Water ingress into the BCM. Recall 18V205 (`recall-18v205`): water corrodes the BCM/A-pillar connector; built 2017-03-20 to 2018-02-14 per the catalogue; remedy is sealing at the A-pillar/HVAC box. Catalogue row is unverified (verified=false). Whether VIN ZASFAKPN5J7B88115 is in scope is not known.
4. Software and configuration mismatch after module flashes. [DOC] NHTSA 08-045-20 (+REV A https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2020/MC-10180265-9999.pdf): C141C-86, B1006-49 and C1999-86 with FCW limited-functionality message; fix is BCM "Restore Vehicle Configuration" then PROXI alignment, clear, remove the MicroPod, let the car sleep 10-15 min with fobs away, re-check. Scope: 2020 Giulia built up to 2019-12-31 and 2020 Stelvio up to 2019-12-20, so NOT a 2018 bulletin. [DOC] STAR S2018000004 (`tsb-star-s2018000004-group`, https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2020/MC-10175670-9999.pdf): after the PCM flash for CSN W05 (`csn-w05`), U04B1-00 "invalid data from battery monitor" sets; do a BCM restore configuration routine, clear, cycle. Models: 2017-2019 Giulia/Stelvio per https://recallcheck.net/tsb/129422 [WEB].
5. ABS software. [DOC] TSB 08-044-19 (`tsb-08-044-19`, https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2019/MC-10162530-9999.pdf): 2018 Stelvio built on or before 2018-03-13 (MDH 0313XX) without ACC (sales code NH3); ABS flash only for brake pedal feel, no DTCs listed; 2 or 3 flash steps with PROXI alignment after step 1 and "restore vehicle" after the final step; charger at 13.2-13.5 V. Not a bulletin for these codes. Applies only if the car is inside the build window and has no ACC.
6. Brake-signal wiring seen from the DASM. [DOC] STAR S2223000047 (`tsb-s2223000047`, https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2022/MC-10221417-9999.pdf): C1452-86 "Engine Torque DASM Signal Invalid"; check brake-switch voltage at PCM pin 71B (under 1 V released, over 11 V pressed), then wiring BSM to ECM/BCM/RFHM, then PCM fail-status. Model years are not in the text. It is a different code from the ones on this car.
7. Battery, charging, IBS. [DOC] only the S2018000004 item above (battery-monitor data after a flash). `tsb-s1408000384` is the IBS/SOC bulletin (2023-02 and later revisions, PDF not opened, no model list read). `tsb-9004130` is a battery bulletin with no technical content in the index row. [WEB] one report says the IBS on the negative terminal can fail and the engine ground strap near the transmission corrodes in road-salt markets, worst on 2017-2018 cars; the source page was not opened, so quality unknown. [FORUM] generic: a low-voltage crank is when CAN drops first (Fiat forum thread cited in section 1).
8. Aftermarket devices. [DOC] SGW note above: aftermarket messages are blocked silently. [FORUM] Fiat Stilo U1700: removing an aftermarket alarm module and disconnecting the battery cleared it (https://www.fiatforum.com/threads/another-u1700-problem.522484/latest; different car and era). Nothing found tying an OBD-port device to this cluster on Giorgio.
9. Lane keep and camera. Camera obstruction (C141B-97) is by definition 97 (obstructed or blocked): dirty or covered windscreen area, not network. C141C private-CAN code appears in 08-045-20 (2020 only). Japanese 2024-model Giulia/Stelvio cluster-software recall for lane keep (https://www.mlit.go.jp/en/jidosha/content/001991024.pdf, summary only) is out of scope for 2018. DASM/radar/camera alignment procedure for C141B/C141C: not found.
10. Recalls near the modules: 19V148 (`recall-19v148`, BSM software, ACC cannot be disabled after wheel slip, 2018-2019 Stelvio, remedy BSM software update, unverified row); 17V823 contaminated brake fluid (`recall-17v823`). Neither lists a U-code.

## 5. Diagnostic sequence found in the literature

No Giorgio service-manual sequence for U-code plausibility clusters was found. The following is assembled from the sources and is generic where marked.
1. Read all modules, note which are -87 (absent) versus -86/-2F (present but bad data). [WEB] tat.net.au pages above.
2. Battery state of charge and charging voltage, supply and ground at each module. [WEB] tat.net.au U1706/U1711 pages (free preview only); [FORUM] Fiat threads.
3. BCM supply: stand-alone fuse, F82 rear PDC for Stelvio. [DOC] S1808000005.
4. Connector and terminal inspection at the modules reporting. [DOC] S1708000262.
5. Bus resistance: about 60 ohm between CAN-H and CAN-L with ignition off, 58-62 ohm window, about 120 ohm = one terminator missing, near 0 = short, open = both gone. Generic ISO bus test, sources: https://www.aa1car.com/library/can_communication_problem.htm and https://www.youcanic.com/glossary/termination-resistor/. For Giorgio: terminator locations and the expected value at pins 12/13 were not found. Caution [DOC, S1708000246]: the OBD port is on the public side of the SGW, so a reading at pins 6/14 may not represent CAN C. Whether Giorgio pins 6/14 are behind the SGW is not stated in the text read.
6. Software levels: compare module SW with the current wiTECH level; if a module was flashed, run BCM Restore Vehicle Configuration and PROXI alignment (S2018000004, 08-045-20, 08-044-19 text).
7. SGW: confirm wiTECH topology shows modules green; red everywhere means SGW or power (S1708000246).
8. Re-sleep test: key off, doors locked, fobs away, 10-15 min, re-read (08-045-20 text).

## 6. Ranked causes the literature supports for THIS cluster

Basis for ranking: how closely the source's symptom pattern matches several modules flagging intermittent or invalid signals with a clean ABS. Ranking is [INF]; no source gives a probability.

| Rank | Cause | Source | Confirming test |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Unstable supply or ground to the BCM or modules (fuse, power feed, battery, IBS, ground strap) | S1808000005 (`tsb-s1808000005`); S2018000004; Fiat forum threads | Battery load/health test and cranking voltage log; voltage drop on BCM feeds incl. F82 and the stand-alone fuse and grounds, with the car in the failing state |
| 2 | Connector or terminal fault (BCM, ABS, DASM, HALF) | S1708000262 (`tsb-s1708000262`); `tsb-9100226` | Pin-tension and visual check at each connector; wiggle test with live data and CAN error watch |
| 3 | Water in BCM / A-pillar connector | recall 18V205 | VIN recall lookup on NHTSA and Transport Canada, inspect A-pillar connector for corrosion and sealing |
| 4 | Configuration or software mismatch (BCM config, PROXI) after earlier flashes | S2018000004, 08-045-20 text, 08-044-19 text | Compare PROXI and SW levels against current wiTECH list; BCM Restore Vehicle Configuration plus PROXI alignment, then 10-15 min sleep and re-read (dealer-tool step) |
| 5 | CAN physical layer (wiring, terminators) on the chassis bus | generic ISO bus test (aa1car, youcanic) | Resistance with ignition off at the correct bus access point; Giorgio terminator location not found |
| 6 | SGW fault | S1708000246 | Topology view all red; radio behaviour; single SGW DTC per bus |
| 7 | Aftermarket device on OBD port | S1708000246 (silent blocking), Stilo thread | Repeat the scan with every device unplugged |
| 8 | Camera obstruction for C141B-97 only | FTB 97 meaning (Portuguese article) | Inspect and clean the windscreen camera area; alignment procedure not found |
