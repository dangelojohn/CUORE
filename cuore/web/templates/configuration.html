What this is

The Body computer (BCM) holds the car's vehicle configuration. This page finds the block that holds it with read-only requests (service 0x22), saves it for this car, and shows it byte by byte.

What it needs at the car

  1. The CAN-C cable (500 kbps) and the Body computer's confirmed address.
  2. Key on, engine off.
  3. MES closed (CUORE refuses while MES holds the adapter).
  4. Battery support connected. The sweep takes about 20 minutes.

Then confirm the block

Open MES's own PROXI / CAN Setup screen for this car and take a screenshot. Compare its bytes with the candidate's first bytes, then accept the candidate with a reason such as "matches MES CAN Setup screenshot of 2026-10-30". Nothing is called the PROXI block until you accept one.
