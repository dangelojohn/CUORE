Purge-trim EVAP test (passive)

Read-only. Records the ECM's commanded purge %, fuel trims, RPM, MAP, load, coolant, fuel level and ambient temperature for 4 minutes while the engine idles and you touch nothing. The ECM opens the purge valve by itself when its conditions allow; the analysis compares the fuel trims with purge off and on. A canister holding vapour plus a flowing purge path pulls short-term trim negative when the valve opens (inference). The commanded % is the ECM's request, not measured flow; this car has no EVAP pressure sensor. Procedure and limits: docs/maintenance/PURGE_TRIM_TEST.md.
