Trends across visits

Watches values across days and weeks, not inside one session, and flags a value moving toward its limit before a code sets. Only readings taken under the same condition are compared, one point per day, one instrument at a time. Readings that are invalid or suspect are left out and listed with the reason. Nothing here is read from the car now: it all comes from what CUORE and MultiEcuScan already stored.
