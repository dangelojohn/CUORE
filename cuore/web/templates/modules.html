Reachability is physical, not a software limit On Giulia and Stelvio only Bus A (OBD pins 6/14) is reachable with a plain ELM327 — ECM, ZF 8HP, Q4 transfer case, BCM, IPC, RFHUB and PROXI. Bus B (pins 3/11, blue A5 cable) carries comfort and infotainment; Bus C (pins 12/13, grey A6 cable) carries ABS, airbag, electric steering, the forward camera and torque vectoring. No software reaches those without the cable.

Check any adapter does not short pins 1 and 9 — the vendor warns explicitly that this drops the bus on these cars, and many cheap “universal FCA” cables do exactly that.
