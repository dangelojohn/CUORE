Pins 1 and 9 Do not use cables or interfaces with a short between pins 1 and 9 on Giulia or Stelvio; it drops the bus. The green A1 lead ties pins 1/7/9/12 together. Continuity-check any cable before fitting.

CUORE can drive only two of these cables live: A5 blue · blue_a5 A6 grey · grey_a6. Every other row is reference only: CUORE has no route for it, and this page cannot declare a cable (that is done on Live link).
