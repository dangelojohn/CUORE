Complete procedure in priority order. Use a quality DVOM (Fluke 87 or equivalent). All voltage-drop tests run with engine running and electrical loads active (lights on, blower max, brake pressed).
1
Visual inspection — G102 ABS connector and scuttle drain zone
Open hood, driver side. Locate ABS module (behind driver headlight, forward of strut tower). The scuttle drain exits immediately above and behind this area. Inspect:
  • Chassis ground bolt head: bright metal = OK / green-white crust = fail
  • Ring terminal: intact tinned copper = OK / brown-green powder under terminal = fail
  • Wiring loom tape above ABS connector: dry = OK / swollen, wet, or split = fail
  • ABS connector body: no discolouration = OK / white tracking marks on plastic = fail
Pass: bright metal, no crust  |  Fail: any visible corrosion
2
Voltage drop test — G102 under full electrical load
Engine running. Loads active: headlights on, blower max, brake pedal pressed, seat heaters on if equipped. DVOM set to DC millivolts. Negative probe on battery negative post. Positive probe directly on the chassis bolt at G102.
  • < 50mV (0.05V) — excellent, G102 is fine
  • 50–100mV — marginal, clean contact and retest
  • > 100mV — fail, high resistance confirmed, G102 is the root cause
Repeat with probes reversed (chassis to battery neg) to catch ground loops. Any reading above 100mV with probes in either direction = clean this point.
3
Voltage drop test — G103 driver A-pillar base
Pull back driver kick panel trim (2–3 plastic clips). Locate the ring terminal bolt at base of A-pillar body metal, inside the cabin. Same test as Step 2. < 50mV = OK  |  > 80mV = fail
This single reading will confirm or eliminate G103 as the root cause of the driver-side audio and microphone failure. If it reads high, you have isolated the fault — no further modules need to be replaced or replaced.
4
Verify G1 terminal contact faces — both ends
The cable was replaced but the contact surfaces at both bolt ends may have residual oxide from the failed original strap. DVOM in resistance (ohms): probe battery negative to transmission-side bolt head. < 0.1Ω = fine  |  > 0.3Ω = clean both contact faces
To clean: loosen bolt, use 120-grit sandpaper on both the cable terminal and the chassis/transmission metal contact face to bare metal. Apply dielectric grease, re-torque 8–10 Nm.
5
Inspect ABS connector pins
Even if the chassis bolt is clean, the ABS module harness connector can carry independent moisture corrosion. Disconnect the ABS connector. Under flashlight inspect all pins for:
  • Green oxidation on copper pin surfaces
  • White/brown tracking on plastic connector body between pins
  • Swollen or cracked plastic around pin entries (freeze-thaw damage)
Clean: electrical contact cleaner spray, fine brass brush on pin faces, dry with compressed air. Apply Stabilant 22 or CRC 2-26 before reconnecting. Wrap exposed loom with self-amalgamating (silicone) tape, NOT PVC tape — PVC wicks moisture.
6
G101 engine block strap — visual and drop test
Locate short braided strap from engine block to chassis, driver side, near starter. Drop test: battery negative to engine block surface. < 50mV = OK
If G102 and G103 test clean but U1713 (ECM erratic) persists after battery reset, G101 corrosion is the remaining suspect for that specific code.
7
Scuttle drain clearance — prevent recurrence
With hood open, locate driver-side scuttle tray (below windshield, above wiper motor). Run a gentle hose trickle and watch where water exits. It should drain down through a rubber nipple and exit behind the wheel arch liner, not pool near the ABS module.
If water pools near ABS zone: drain is partially blocked or the drain tube has disconnected from its nipple. Clear with compressed air through the wheel arch liner access, or a flexible drain snake from the top. After clearing: add a silicone splash guard above the ABS connector.
Also check: does the Stelvio have a sunroof? If yes, the front-left drain nipple exits in the driver A-pillar area. Inspect it with the sunroof open — there should be a rubber hose with a slit tip. If the slit is clogged with pine needle debris (common in Canadian spring), water backs up and runs directly down the A-pillar to G103.
8
Battery disconnect reset — after all physical repairs
After all ground points cleaned and drain confirmed clear: disconnect battery negative for 20 minutes minimum. Reconnect. Start engine. Turn steering wheel lock-to-lock twice slowly (resets some ADAS module sleep states). Let idle 5 minutes. Re-scan with Launch or AlfaOBD.
Expected result: all stored U-codes and B-codes will not return. Any code that returns after this reset points to a remaining hardware issue.
The one code that will NOT self-clear regardless of ground repair: B10AA-4A (PROXI configuration — incorrect component installed). This requires AlfaOBD software or Witech 2.0 at a dealer to reset. It is a software log entry caused by the ground event, not an ongoing hardware problem.
